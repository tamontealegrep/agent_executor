## Conventional fertility treatments (IVF) in Bogotá

All in vitro fertilization treatments are offered exclusively in Bogotá, Colombia. The person must be able to travel to Bogotá to do the process. If they cannot travel, this route is not viable for now, and the conversation can be resumed if their situation changes.

### In vitro fertilization (IVF)

IVF is a high-complexity assisted reproduction technique in which eggs and sperm are joined in the laboratory to form embryos, which are then transferred to the uterus. Depending on the case, it can be done with the person's own genetic material or with donated material.

### IVF with donated eggs

It is used when the ovarian reserve is compromised, or when there is a genetic condition that the patient could transmit and it is advisable to work with donation. In that case Family Aims works with its egg donation program.

### ROPA method (dual motherhood)

The ROPA method is an assisted reproduction treatment for couples of women. One partner provides the eggs and the other carries the pregnancy, so both take part biologically in the process. The ROPA method is only available for couples of women.

### Options by profile

- Heterosexual couple, single woman, and other profiles: traditional IVF and IVF with donated eggs.
- Couple of women: the ROPA method, traditional IVF and IVF with donated eggs.
- Couple of men or a single man: IVF alone is not a path Family Aims offers, because it does not include an egg donor or a surrogate. The surrogacy program does cover that case.

### How long IVF takes

- IVF with stimulation in Colombia: between 20 and 30 days, including the egg extraction.
- IVF when the patient is already stimulated: between 10 and 15 days.
- In IVF treatments the second phase is the embryo transfer.

## Surrogacy

### What it is

Surrogacy (gestational surrogacy) is an assisted reproduction process in which a woman, called the surrogate, carries a pregnancy for a person or couple who wishes to become parents and cannot carry it themselves, for example because of a medical condition. Through IVF an embryo is created and transferred to the surrogate's uterus. The surrogate does not provide her eggs, so there is no genetic link between her and the baby. The exact path depends on the case and on the suitable country.

### Where it is available

Surrogacy programs are available in Colombia, Mexico and Georgia, with different alternatives depending on each family's needs and profile. Mexico is an approved destination for selected profiles, including cases where the Colombia path is not the best legal fit.

### 360° support

- Donor selection and embryo formation.
- Surrogate matching and embryo transfer.
- Support during pregnancy, birth and the legal path.
- Weekly updates and care in Spanish and English, with support in Mandarin when applicable.

### Programs

There are two types of programs: the Program with Financial Guarantee and the Program without Financial Guarantee. In both cases the scope ranges from medical services to obtaining the baby's civil birth certificate.

Program with Financial Guarantee:
- Repeats the necessary medical procedures within the program timeframe.
- Seeks to achieve pregnancy within 24 months.
- If the delay depends on the process or on clinical factors, the program is extended until the service delivery is completed.
- Admission depends on the sperm quality validated by the laboratory.

Program without Financial Guarantee:
- Has a fixed number of selected transfers.
- If pregnancy is not achieved and the person wishes to continue, it requires additional payment.
- If there are already embryos formed, the additional cost corresponds to the transfers.
- If there are no embryos, a new aspiration, embryo formation or a change of surrogate may be required, depending on the case.

### How long surrogacy takes

The estimated duration of the process is 24 months, although it may vary according to each case.

### Country-specific conditions

- Colombia: at least one of the intended parents must provide genetic material. Because of this, the destination that fits a single woman, or a woman without a partner, is Mexico.
- Mexico: double donation can exist, provided that the country of origin does not require a DNA test for registration. Single women's processes can be accompanied there.
- Colombia, migration: to take part in a surrogacy program in Colombia, the intended parents must have a migration status that allows them to enter and remain legally in Colombia for the time needed to complete the process. Depending on nationality, entry may depend on holding a valid USA or Schengen visa. This policy responds exclusively to Colombian migration requirements and aims to make sure the process can be carried out legally and safely. If the Colombia path is not viable for someone, it is only because of those migration requirements and never because of their family project. The team reviews each case.

## What Family Aims does not do

- It does not offer adoptions. Its work focuses on fertility treatments and surrogacy.
- It does not handle applications from women who want to be surrogates or gestational carriers.
- It does not give detailed medical or legal advice through this channel. Specific details are reviewed with the expert team.
- It does not promise medical or legal outcomes, approval, or timelines beyond the approved information. No medical or legal program can guarantee absolute results, and each case is analyzed individually.
- The assistant does not quote prices. Costs depend on the program and on each case, and the human advisor explains values and the specifics of the case.
