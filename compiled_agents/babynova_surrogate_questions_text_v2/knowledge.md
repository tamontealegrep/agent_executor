## The surrogacy program

The surrogate gestational program allows women to help other people who cannot gestate their own baby. The woman who carries the pregnancy is called a gestational mother or surrogate (gestante).

Taking part in the program is never guaranteed at the first contact. It depends on a later evaluation by the BabyNova team.

## Steps of the process

1. Text pre-qualification: the virtual assistant asks a short set of questions (about 5 minutes) to check that the candidate meets the initial criteria.
2. Appointment with the BabyNova team: candidates who pass the pre-qualification schedule a short appointment of about 10 minutes, in person at the clinic. The team reviews the case and answers questions.
3. Medical and psychological evaluation: candidates who continue are summoned for a full in-person evaluation.
4. Legal and medical process: approved candidates begin the formal surrogacy process accompanied by the BabyNova team.

The final selection criteria are determined in the full medical and psychological evaluation. The process to be a gestational mother includes medical, psychological and legal evaluation.

## Initial requirements (pre-qualification)

The pre-qualification checks these basic criteria:

- Age: between 18 and 38 years old.
- Residence: live in one of the enabled municipalities: Bogotá, Soacha, Zipaquirá, Chía, Mosquera, Funza, Cajicá, Madrid, La Calera, Cota, Facatativá and Sibaté.
- Children: have between one and four children born alive.
- Last birth: enough time must have passed since the last birth. After a C-section, at least 18 months. After a natural birth, at least 12 months.
- C-sections: no more than two C-sections.
- Abortions: having had an abortion does not exclude a candidate by itself. The information is recorded for the medical evaluation.
- Preeclampsia: a history of preeclampsia excludes the candidate from the process.
- Body mass index (BMI): within the accepted range, calculated from weight and height.
- Psychoactive substances: the candidate must not use them.
- Time: the candidate must have time available to attend the medical appointments.
- Identity document: only the Cédula de Ciudadanía (CC) or the Permiso por Protección Temporal (PPT) are accepted.

Nationality is asked and recorded, but it is not a reason for exclusion by itself. Meeting these criteria does not guarantee participation: the final decision comes after the full evaluation.
